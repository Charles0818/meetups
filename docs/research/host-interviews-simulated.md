# Host Interviews — Simulated Discovery Pass

> **⚠️ Not real field data.** These are synthetic organizer personas generated to pressure-test the
> recurring-host thesis before real interviews. They stand in for E0.T1 but do **not** satisfy it.
> Treat every quote as a hypothesis to confirm, never as evidence. Real interviews remain the gate
> before any paid-tier engineering.

**Date:** 2026-08-27 · **Method:** simulated role-play · **Personas:** 5 recurring meetup organizers

Two core questions asked of each:

1. What did you do the last time you needed to collect money for an event?
2. What would you be most upset to lose if your current tool vanished tomorrow?
   Plus reactions to the three bets: chat-native RSVP · the no-show fix (confirm-or-release / deposit) · the persistent list.

---

## P1 — Marta · weekly trail run club · ~30 on WhatsApp · free · no budget

- **Money:** none. Once-a-year T-shirt order, collected by posting her account number and ticking names off transfer screenshots. Tolerable only because it's annual.
- **Would hate to lose:** the **group chat itself** — "that's the club." Hard requirement: RSVP must live _inside_ WhatsApp; moving the conversation out = never adopts.
- **No-show:** brutal (~22 react, ~9 show). Wants "N people actually coming Saturday." Confirm-or-release: yes. Deposit: no — free club.
- **Pay:** not for the app. Would pay small (Strava-like) _if_ turnout measurably improves. Free until it works.

## P2 — Tobi · monthly JS/frontend meetup · ~65 RSVP / ~35 show · sponsor-funded

- **Money:** from a **sponsor**, via DM + invoice. Attendees free, always. Tool touches none of it.
- **Would hate to lose:** already on **Luma**, content with it. "Lighter" won't move him.
- **No-show:** the gap is a **catering cost** — orders pizza to the RSVP number, wastes it. A day-before confirm giving a real cater-to number is worth real money.
- **Pay:** yes, specifically for the cater-to-number/no-show fix. But he's the hardest to move — has a working tool. **Signals tech meetups are NOT the beachhead.**

## P3 — Deng · monthly board-game night · venue seats 12 · small cover

- **Money:** charges a cover (room costs him), collects by **transfer + screenshot**, reconciles in the group chat at 11pm, chases forgetters. Hates it.
- **Would hate to lose:** the **waitlist** — wants the next person auto-pulled when someone drops (currently manual).
- **No-show:** capacity-capped, so a **small deposit is _fair_, not unfriendly** — flakes self-select out. The one persona where deposits are welcome.
- **Pay:** "take a cut of the cover, I don't care" — just wants to stop screenshot accounting. **The paying/payments case.**

## P4 — Priya · fortnightly community young-adults group · 80–150 · WhatsApp + Google Form

- **Money:** event free 90% of the time. Twice-yearly retreat has a fee → Google Form + transfers + hand-reconciled spreadsheet = chaos.
- **Would hate to lose:** **the list.** Four years of people scattered across dead broadcasts and forms, no home. Holding it + messaging it + surfacing who's drifted = "the whole job."
- **No-show:** present but secondary to the list.
- **Pay:** would pay monthly for the **list** without blinking. Zero for a prettier page. **The clearest list-as-asset signal.**

## P5 — Sam · monthly supper club · 16 seats · free, hosts eats the cost

- **Money:** fronts food, asks for Venmo _after_, ~half pay, stopped asking — "chasing friends is gross." Wants an **optional contribution up front at RSVP** to remove the chase.
- **Would hate to lose:** less list-dependent (small, already in phone). No-show is the real pain — 4 ghosts deflates a 16-seat night.
- **No-show:** confirm-or-release yes. Deposit among friends: no — too transactional for a dinner.
- **Pay:** free tier for RSVP + confirm; happy to pay when the money-collection part is wanted.
